The best cost-per-lead we have reported on this account. 64 leads at $49.57 each, against $74.53 in Aug 1–14 and $85.88 in July. Two things to keep in mind while reading the rest of this report. First, the improvement started before the new site went live — the Aug 16–20 window, still on the old site, was already at $51.56. Second, on Aug 20 the phone-call conversion was changed to count calls from 25 seconds instead of 60, so calls between 25 and 59 seconds now register as leads where they previously did not. That change affects every day from Aug 20 onward and we cannot separate its effect from the site's with the data we have. "Lead Conversions" = tracked phone-call leads + lead-form submits, the primary actions we optimize for.
