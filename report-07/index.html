Data Report · September 1–30, 2026Relatório de Dados · 1–30 Set, 2026

This is a straight data report for the full month of September — and the last one on the current campaign structure. The rebuild approved in September replaces it next, and the numbers below become the starting line it is measured against. Headline: 57 leads at $96.37 per lead on $5,493.10 of spend. That is the highest cost per lead of the five months we have on record (May–September), and about half the lead pace of late August. Clicks cost roughly the same as in August; what changed is how many of them became a call or a form — 6.04% of clicks, down from 11.23% in Aug 16–31. The section “What Changed in September” looks at why, and carries the one question this month where only you have the answer.

The conversion rate fell by about half almost everywhere at once. Driveways, Patios and Outdoor Living all went from 9–13% to 4–7%. The clearest case is the line that changed least: people searching maxima concrete in the Brand Protection campaign — same search, same ad, same landing page as in August — converted at 15.62% in late August and 3.95% in September. Searches with your name as a whole halved too (17.39% → 8.64%), while the number of those clicks per day stayed about the same (5.75 → 6.17). The one exception is Competitor Targeting, which went the other way (7.32% → 13.87%). The cost per click barely moved anywhere. A keyword or budget change would show up in some campaigns and not others, and would not touch people who already searched for you by name. A drop this even across the account points past the click: either fewer of the people who reached the site went on to call or send a form, or some of those calls and forms happened and were not recorded. The ad platform cannot tell those two apart — your own count of September calls and requests can.A taxa de conversão caiu mais ou menos pela metade em quase tudo ao mesmo tempo. Driveways, Patios e Outdoor Living saíram de 9–13% para 4–7%. O caso mais claro é a linha que menos mudou: quem buscou maxima concrete na campanha Brand Protection — mesma busca, mesmo anúncio, mesma página de destino de Agosto — converteu 15.62% no fim de Agosto e 3.95% em Setembro. As buscas com o seu nome como um todo também caíram pela metade (17.39% → 8.64%), enquanto o número desses cliques por dia ficou praticamente igual (5.75 → 6.17). A única exceção é a Competitor Targeting, que foi para o outro lado (7.32% → 13.87%). O custo por clique quase não se mexeu em lugar nenhum. Uma mudança de keyword ou de orçamento apareceria em algumas campanhas e não em outras, e não afetaria quem já buscou você pelo nome. Uma queda tão uniforme na conta aponta para depois do clique: ou menos pessoas que chegaram ao site seguiram para ligar ou mandar formulário, ou parte dessas ligações e formulários aconteceu e não foi registrada. A plataforma de anúncios não consegue diferenciar uma coisa da outra — a sua contagem de ligações e pedidos de Setembro consegue.

One number that is consistent with the site still being used: the lighter, secondary interactions Google also tracks held steady at about 36 for every 100 clicks, the same as in late August.
